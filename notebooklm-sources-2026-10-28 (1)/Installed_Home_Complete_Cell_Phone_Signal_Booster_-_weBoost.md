### Frequently Asked Questions

Will the weBoost Installed | Home Complete boost my home internet?

No, not directly. As a cellular signal booster, the weBoost Installed | Home Complete will only boost home internet and Wi-Fi when it's connected through a cellular modem or hotspot.

Can I buy the weBoost Installed | Home Complete without installation?

Yes. While the weBoost Installed | Home Complete includes our professional installation, you can purchase our Home Complete and install the booster yourself.

Will the installer install the weBoost Installed | Home Complete in a commercial building or business?

No. The weBoost Installed | Home Complete is for residential use only. Our installers do not provide installation services in businesses or commercial buildings. You can purchase the Home Complete for DIY installation, or you can check out our commercial-grade solutions on WilsonPro.com.

Do you offer professional installation to areas outside the United States?

No. The weBoost Installed | Home Complete is only available to residents within the United States. Our certified technicians can fulfill installation requests to any address in any U.S. zip code. For DIY installation, we offer different home booster models for Canada. Any other countries are incompatible with boosters sold through weBoost.com.

Can I add coax cable to the weBoost Installed | Home Complete after installation?

No. You won't need to add additional cable once the weBoost Installed | Home Complete is professionally installed in your home. Because our certified technicians provide customized installation, they will cut the coax cable to the correct length at the time of installation.

Can the weBoost Installed | Home Complete technician install more than one inside and outside antenna?

No. Our installers will only install the antennas included in the weBoost Installed | Home Complete kit. Any additional inside and outside antennas will need to be installed by the homeowner.

How do I install a weBoost cell phone signal booster?

To install a weBoost booster kit, download the weBoost app. Designed to provide guided installation instructions, videos, and information to help you install the outside antenna, booster, inside antenna, and signal-carrying cables. Follow the step-by-step instructions to install your booster the right way, the first time. The app will start by measuring your current cell signal, then finalize installation by showing how much your coverage has improved. The proof is in the app.

Not enough cell signal or internet to download the app before install?

Don't worry. We include a printed installation guide with every booster kit.
