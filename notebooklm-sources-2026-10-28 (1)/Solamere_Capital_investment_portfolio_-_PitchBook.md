Founded in 2008, Solamere Capital is a private equity firm based in Boston, Massachusetts. The firm seeks to invest in aerospace, defense, automotive, food & beverage, professional services, infrastructure, utility services, residential services, environmental services, and advanced manufacturing sectors. This firm is a Registered Investment Adviser (RIA).
