## Inline surge protection for 50-ohm booster systems

The SureCall SC-LP is a 50-ohm coaxial lightning and surge protector designed for compatible cellular signal-booster antenna lines. Installed between the outdoor antenna feed and protected equipment, it provides a grounded discharge path that helps divert transient electrical energy away from the booster.

Its N-Female connectors integrate with common 50-ohm N-type coax runs, while insertion loss below 0.2 dB helps preserve RF performance across frequencies up to 3 GHz. It is a protection component—not a substitute for proper bonding, grounding, cable entry practices, or code-compliant installation.

**50-ohm RF path** Built for compatible commercial and residential booster systems using N-type coax.

**Low insertion loss** Less than 0.2 dB attenuation minimizes impact on the planned signal path.

**Outdoor-ready housing** Weather-resistant construction supports properly protected antenna-line installations.

### Protect the equipment side of the antenna run

The protector should be positioned at the building entry or another approved grounding location and bonded to the site grounding system using methods established by the qualified installer. Final placement depends on the system design, local electrical requirements, cable routing, and grounding architecture.

## Design & Installation

**Confirm system compatibility.** Verify a 50-ohm RF system, compatible N-type connections, supported frequency range, and the correct location in the antenna feed line.

**Select the grounding point.** The qualified installer determines placement near the cable entry and coordinates bonding with the building grounding-electrode system.

**Install in the coax path.** Keep the connection mechanically supported, protect cable bend radius, and avoid loading the protector or RF ports.

**Bond the protector.** Use an appropriately sized grounding conductor and approved hardware in accordance with applicable electrical codes and project requirements.

**Weatherproof exterior connections.** Seal outdoor RF connections using the specified professional weatherproofing method.

**Test the completed path.** Verify continuity, grounding, connector torque, signal levels, and system operation before commissioning.

**Professional installation:** Lightning protection and grounding must be designed and installed by qualified personnel. No surge-protection device can guarantee protection from every direct or indirect lightning event.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where should the SC-LP be installed?

It is typically placed in the outdoor-antenna coax path near the building entry or another approved grounding location selected by the system designer or qualified installer.

### Does it replace grounding and bonding?

No. The protector must be properly bonded into the site grounding system and used with code-compliant grounding and cable-entry practices.

### Will it reduce booster performance?

Its specified attenuation is less than 0.2 dB, but the complete RF path should still be included in the system link-budget calculation.

### Does it fit 75-ohm systems?

No. The SC-LP is a 50-ohm device with N-Female connectors. Use components specifically designed for 75-ohm systems when required.

### Can it guarantee protection from lightning?

No device can guarantee protection from every lightning event. It is one part of a professionally designed grounding, bonding, and surge-protection strategy.
