## Description

The WilsonPro 1000C is the first amplifier that incorporates the powerful WilsonPro Cloud Service, allowing this powerful enterprise booster to be alarmed, monitored, and configured remotely.

The Pro 1000C communicates back to the WilsonPro Cloud Service wirelessly using a built-in LTE connection. Alternatively it can be connected over a hardwired ethernet connection.

The amplifier itself is very similar to the [WilsonPro 1000](https://www.waveform.com/products/wilson-pro-1000), with the primary difference being the addition of the WilsonPro Cloud feature set. The Pro 1000C comes with a 1-year subscription to WilsonPro Cloud service.

## Top Pick for Enterprise Boosters

We've chosen the WilsonPro 1000C as one of our top enterprise signal boosters in our [Cell Phone Signal Booster Guide](https://www.waveform.com/pages/cell-phone-signal-booster-guide). Here's why:

WilsonPro Cloud Service is one of the most robust monitoring solutions available, and puts this unit in a class above competing units.

Built-in LTE modem makes monitoring setup easy, even when there's no ethernet connectivity.

17 dBm downlink output power is best-in-class for this type of signal booster, making the WilsonPro 1000C ideally suited for larger buildings.

## WilsonPro Cloud Service

The WilsonPro Cloud Service allows you to remotely control and monitor your WilsonPro 1000C, including allowing you to:

Remotely reset the amplifier

Enable and disable specific frequency bands

Generate reports & view performance histories

Organize monitored amplifiers by location

Use the antenna tuning tool to optimize outdoor antenna location and direction

Additionally, the WilsonPro Cloud Service allows you to set up customizable email and SMS text alert notifications for the following events:

Amplifier offline or unable to communicate with Cloud Service

Oscillation (feedback) detected

Weak or no signal detected

The WilsonPro 1000C connects to the WilsonPro Cloud using either a built-in LTE modem, or through an Ethernet port on the bottom of the device. The first year of WilsonPro cloud service is included with the device.

## 5 Critical Tips for the WilsonPro 1000C:

### 1. Take signal measurements before you buy

The WilsonPro 1000C can cover up to an area of up to 30,000 square feet, but only if your outdoor signal is strong enough. This isn't true for just the Pro 1000C, but across the board for all signal boosters.

We strongly recommend [measuring your outdoor signal](https://www.waveform.com/pages/cell-phone-signal-booster-guide#guide-measurements) and **speaking to our team of Signal Experts** before you purchase. Our Signal Experts can help guide you to the best solution based on your signal conditions.

### 2. Getting your outdoor antenna positioned correctly is critical

As we explain in our [Signal Booster Guide](https://www.waveform.com/pages/cell-phone-signal-booster-guide#guide-strength-quality), the bars on your phone are more than just signal strength. Signal quality is absolutely critical.

The only way to improve the signal quality coming into your booster is getting the outdoor antenna positioned correctly. Our team of Signal Experts can help guide you as you install the Pro 1000C.

### 3. Don't need remote monitoring? Go with the Pro 1000

While the WilsonPro 1000C's remote monitoring can be a huge benefit to some, it's not always critical. If there's an issue with your signal booster, and you're in the building, you'll generally notice the signal degradation pretty quickly. If you're not concerned about remote monitoring your system, you can save money by purchasing the [WilsonPro 1000](https://www.waveform.com/products/wilson-pro-1000).

### 4. Consider the Cel-Fi QUATRA 2000

The [Cel-Fi QUATRA 2000](https://www.waveform.com/products/cel-fi-quatra-2000-signal-booster-system) targets a similar market to the WilsonPro line, however it is a carrier-specific system that the [FCC allows](https://www.waveform.com/pages/cell-phone-signal-booster-guide#guide-regulations) to utilize up to 100 decibels gain. The extra gain means the QUATRA works better in remote areas with very weak outdoor signal. The QUATRA also uses ethernet cable in place of coaxial cable, and has it's own robust remote monitoring built-in.

It's not all upside, though. The QUATRA 2000 has both benefits and disadvantages. For example, each unit only works with 2 carriers at a time, so you need two sets for 4-carrier coverage. Additionally, installation can be a little more complicated than the WilsonPro products.

### 5. The WilsonPro 1000C is designed to be part of a custom-designed system

There's a reason that Wilson doesn't allow these units to be sold online: they're supposed to be designed into a custom system, with multiple antennas, splitters, couplers, and custom-length cables.

We offer complimentary design services using the industry-standard iBwave in-building design software. Simply [contact our Signal Specialists](https://www.waveform.com/pages/contact-us) and we'll chat through your building and requirements and help put together a custom solution for you.

## About the WilsonPro 1000C

The WilsonPro 1000C is a passive distributed antenna system that boosts signal for all major US carriers in large buildings.

The Pro 1000C is amongst the most powerful FCC-certified signal boosters available in the US, making it ideally suited for larger buildings, including:

Large homes

Commercial spaces and retail

Offices

Hospitals

Hotels

Warehouses

The Pro 1000C is amongst the most feature-rich and powerful amplifiers we sell. It's list of features include:

**Up to 17 dBm downlink output power** [Downlink output power](https://www.waveform.com/pages/cell-phone-signal-booster-guide#guide-gain-and-dl-power) defines the maximum coverage area of a booster system when outdoor signal is strong. The FCC [limits](https://www.waveform.com/pages/cell-phone-signal-booster-guide#guide-regulations) broadband signal boosters to no more than 17 dBm downlink output power, and the Pro 1000C maxes out that specification. No other booster we sell offers a larger coverage area, except multiple-output units like the [WilsonPro 4000](https://www.waveform.com/products/wilson-pro-4000), [4000R](https://www.waveform.com/products/wilsonpro-4000r), and [Cel-Fi Quatra](https://www.waveform.com/products/cel-fi-quatra-2000-signal-booster-system).

**Up to 70 dB gain** The [gain specification](https://www.waveform.com/pages/cell-phone-signal-booster-guide#guide-gain-and-dl-power) of an amplifier describes the amount that it can amplify signal. If outdoor signal is weak, the gain of the amplifier and outdoor antenna defines the total coverage area. The FCC [limits](https://www.waveform.com/pages/cell-phone-signal-booster-guide#guide-regulations) the gain of broadband signal boosters to between 64 and 72 dB, depending on the frequency band.

**eXtended Dynamic Range (XDR) technology** WilsonPro's XDR technology allows its amplifiers to dynamically adjust gain to ensure that amplifiers are never overpowered. That means that even if a carrier puts a tower up directly next door, the WilsonPro 1000C will stay online.
