### Frequently Asked Questions

What is a DAS System?

A **Distributed Antenna System (DAS)** is a network of antennas connected to a shared signal source to improve indoor cellular coverage. DAS solutions boost weak mobile signals in buildings—making them ideal for areas where dropped calls, slow data, or no signal are common. If you're asking how to fix signal issues in a commercial or industrial space, DAS is one of the most effective answers.

Why Use Passive DAS?

Reliable indoor mobile signal is essential for both **business operations** and **visitor experience**. Employees depend on strong signal for calls, emails, cloud access, and apps. Guests often need connectivity to access **tickets, reservations, or mobile vouchers**, check in with family, or browse social media.

A **Passive DAS** system, like the A8000, captures the outdoor cellular signal and distributes it indoors via coaxial cable. It's a cost-effective solution that works across all major networks and is ideal for buildings up to **8,000 m²**.

What If Signal Problems Are Limited to One Carrier?

Signal strength issues can happen when a carrier's tower is either too far or too close to the building. You can't control what mobile network your staff or visitors use—but a Passive DAS system like the A8000 helps bridge the gap.

If certain areas of the building require **multi-network** coverage and others need **carrier-specific optimization**, the A8000 supports channel-specific tuning for more precise signal enhancement.

Passive vs Active vs Hybrid DAS – What's the Difference?

**Passive DAS** (like the A8000): Uses outdoor signal captured by an antenna, distributed indoors over coax cable. Best for mid-sized commercial buildings, warehouses, and offices.

How much coverage does the WilsonPro A8000 provide?

The WilsonPro A8000 can cover **up to 8,000 square metres**, depending on the strength of the incoming signal, building layout, and internal materials. It's ideal for medium to large-scale commercial environments looking to eliminate dropped calls and weak data zones.

What kind of antennas are used with the WilsonPro A8000?

The A8000 works with high-gain **outdoor donor antennas** (like LPDA or Omni) and **indoor broadcast antennas** (ceiling domes or wall panels). The antenna configuration depends on your building's structure and coverage needs, ensuring optimal performance and coverage.

Is professional installation required for the A8000?

Yes, due to its advanced capabilities and commercial-scale design, the WilsonPro A8000 **requires professional installation**. This ensures correct placement of antennas, optimal cable runs, and maximum signal performance with minimal interference.

Can the WilsonPro A8000 be monitored remotely?

Yes. The A8000 includes **WilsonPro Cloud** remote monitoring, allowing real-time performance tracking, diagnostics, and system alerts through a secure online dashboard - ideal for IT teams managing multiple sites or large facilities.
