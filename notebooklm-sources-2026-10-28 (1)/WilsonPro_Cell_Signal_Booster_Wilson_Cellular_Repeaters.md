## [WilsonPro Enterprise 1398](https://powerfulsignal.com/professional-design-install/wilsonpro-product-series/wilsonpro-enterprise-1398)

WilsonPro Enterprise 1398 wall-mount (top) and rack-mount (bottom)

The wall- or rack-mounted [WilsonPro Enterprise 1398](https://powerfulsignal.com/professional-design-install/wilsonpro-product-series/wilsonpro-enterprise-1398) amplifies 3.45–3.98 GHz C-band cellular signal, providing fast 5G data coverage for commercial, industrial, and other large indoor spaces.

The Enterprise 1398 automatically synchronizes with band n77 networks and supports a 2×2 MIMO or a SISO implementation with up to 400 MHz total bandwidth.

The Enterprise 1398 features optional WilsonPro Cloud remote monitoring and control.

## [WilsonPro Enterprise 4330](https://powerfulsignal.com/professional-design-install/wilsonpro-product-series/wilsonpro-enterprise-4330)

WilsonPro Enterprise 4330 wall-mount (top) and rack-mount (bottom)

The wall- or rack-mounted [WilsonPro Enterprise 4330](https://powerfulsignal.com/professional-design-install/wilsonpro-product-series/wilsonpro-enterprise-4330) has four independent amplifiers like the Enterprise 4300 but features **channelized capability**, allowing specific blocks ( *channels*) within a band of cellular frequency to be amplified or disabled.
