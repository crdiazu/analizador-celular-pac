### WilsonPro FAQs

What makes WilsonPro signal boosters stand out in terms of performance and reliability?

WilsonPro signal boosters use precision-tuned components and patented technology like XDR (eXtended Dynamic Range), ensuring consistent signal performance even in challenging environments.

How does WilsonPro handle signal overload and fluctuations better than other boosters?

With built-in XDR technology, WilsonPro boosters automatically adjust to prevent signal overload, ensuring uninterrupted performance even in areas with variable outside signal strength.

Are WilsonPro boosters more scalable than other signal boosting systems?

Yes. WilsonPro systems can be tailored from single-room setups to enterprise-grade buildings, allowing seamless scalability without replacing the core booster.

Do WilsonPro boosters provide remote monitoring and control?

Absolutely. The WilsonPro Cloud platform enables real-time remote monitoring, alerts, diagnostics, and performance adjustments, which many competing systems do not offer natively.

Is WilsonPro more suitable for commercial and industrial installations?

Yes. With models like the WilsonPro A1000 and Enterprise A8000, WilsonPro is specifically designed to meet the high-demand needs of large facilities, warehouses, and multi-storey buildings.

How does WilsonPro ensure compatibility with all South African networks?

Our carefully selected range of WilsonPro boosters are fully carrier-agnostic and certified to work across Vodacom, MTN, Cell C, Telkom,, and other regional operators, with multi-band support built-in.

How does WilsonPro deliver a better return on investment (ROI)?

With no recurring fees, durable hardware, and scalable design, WilsonPro boosters offer long-term value and reduced total cost of ownership compared to less advanced alternatives.

What is Automatic Gain Control (AGC) in WilsonPro Signal Boosters and why is it important?

Automatic Gain Control (AGC) allows WilsonPro boosters to **automatically adjust signal strength** based on the outdoor signal environment. This ensures **optimal performance** without manual intervention, even if the signal fluctuates throughout the day. AGC protects the system from overloading and helps maintain strong, consistent indoor coverage.

How does AGC improve the performance and longevity of the signal booster system?

AGC ensures the amplifier never pushes itself beyond safe operating levels, which **extends the system's lifespan** and reduces the risk of dropped calls, overheating, or system shutdowns. It's a key reason WilsonPro boosters are chosen for mission-critical environments like corporate buildings and public infrastructure.

Why is 'plug-and-play' performance important for commercial users?

Plug-and-play functionality reduces installation time and disruption to business operations. For companies moving into new office space or expanding rapidly, WilsonPro boosters provide **immediate improvements to voice and data connectivity,** without waiting for infrastructure upgrades.
