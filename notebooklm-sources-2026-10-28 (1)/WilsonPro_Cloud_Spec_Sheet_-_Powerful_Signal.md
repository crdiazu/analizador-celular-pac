# **WILSONPRO CLOUD**

The industry’s first platform for

**cloud-based management & monitoring
**of cellular signal amplifiers.

**The WilsonPro Cloud **allows an integrator to
manage and monitor installed cellular amplifiers
from a phone, tablet, laptop, or any device that
runs a Web browser. You can get customizable
email and text notifications to alert you to any
status change of your installed amplifiers,
including notification if a system ever goes offline.

With the WilsonPro Cloud you can remotely reset
an amp or selectively turn specific frequency
bands on and off, so the integrator avoids costly
troubleshooting site visits. The platform also
provides report generation, performance and
signal level histories, and organization of
monitored amps by account and location. There’s
even a remote Donor (outside) antenna tuning tool.
