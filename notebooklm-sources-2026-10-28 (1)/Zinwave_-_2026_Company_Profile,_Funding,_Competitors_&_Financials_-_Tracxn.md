## Zinwave - About the company

Zinwave is an acquired company based in Cambridge (United Kingdom), founded in 2002. It operates as a Distributed antenna system (DAS) technology provider for in-building wireless coverage. Zinwave has raised $21.9M in funding from [SEB Venture Capital](https://tracxn.com/d/venture-capital/seb-venture-capital/__RfXP_Vd7zpQphOLFlRpahB6uIJaar8AKhW8JHRh4ock). The company has 165 active competitors, including 19 funded and 44 that have exited. Its top competitors include companies like [Airspan](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/airspan/__faOgsea-suvENNPre0alNTNlJPV1TfZEjmn9Lc1bjTQ), [Celona](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/celona/__HjsLsZgNx110IO_ECp0ygNQ7_k0EK_K2dewSNCBJZs0) and [Gapwaves](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/gapwaves/__U-DK6v3Au5_5dNCl2Iyw9ZVtAcFAmerlXx8Qzuikdq0).

### Company Details

Distributed antenna system (DAS) technology provider for in-building wireless coverage. It is a provider of wideband, PIM resistant, Full MIMO, TDD, and FDD active distributed antenna systems for in-building wireless coverage. It has a layered system for in-building wireless and IP applications. It also provides professional services, training, consultancy, and support to facilitate building projects.
