Man Machine Interface or MMI codes are codes you can enter into your phone's dial pad to perform various functions. Supplementary Service (SS) and Unstructured Supplementary Service Data (USSD) communicate with your phone's carrier and can display information or enable certain service-related features. There are also manufacturer-specific codes that display information about your device and enable or disable certain features. This wikiHow article teaches you all the MMI codes for [Android](https://www.wikihow.com/Turn-On-an-Android-Phone) you could ever need. [[1]](https://www.wikihow.com/Android-Secret-Codes#_note-1)XResearchsource

## How to Use MMI Codes for Android

To Use MMI codes on your Android, open the **Phone** app and display the dial pad. Enter the code and tap the Call button as though you are making a call. MMI codes usually start with an asterisk (*). Some codes may not work on all devices.
